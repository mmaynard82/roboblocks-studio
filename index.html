🔌 Hook up your robot
Arduino Uno/NanoL298N motor driver2 DC motorsExternal motor battery
  1. Connect L298N IN1, IN2, IN3, IN4 to Arduino digital pins 8, 9, 10, 11.
  2. Connect L298N ENA, ENB to PWM pins 5, 6; remove driver ENA/ENB jumpers for PWM speed control.
  3. Connect left motor to OUT1/OUT2 and right motor to OUT3/OUT4.
  4. Power motors with an appropriately rated external battery, connect Arduino GND to driver GND, and follow the motor driver's power specifications. Never drive motors directly from Arduino pins.
  5. Download your .ino sketch, open it in Arduino IDE, select your board/port, then upload.
Optional: ultrasonic obstacle block uses HC-SR04 TRIG D12 and ECHO D7 (5V Uno/Nano). For safety, test with the robot's wheels lifted first. This starter assumes an L298N and Uno/Nano; other hardware needs different wiring/code.
